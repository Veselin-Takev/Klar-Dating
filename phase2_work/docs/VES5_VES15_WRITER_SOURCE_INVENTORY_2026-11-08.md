## Mandatory closing protocol (still unimplemented)

1. **Enumerate and freeze all writers** that can change a verification, legal hold, object generation, account-deletion state or bucket IAM. For every actor collect authority, tenant/project, staging/prod identity, API entrypoint, triggers, retry and kill switch. Keep Console/emergency procedures in scope.
2. **Approve the legal ordering** for deletion and hold revocation. A Storage-native hold that is set and acknowledged before declaring a Firestore legal hold may be one design component, but it cannot be presumed atomic; failures must be durably reported and writer ordering independently tested. Do not silently claim a legal hold was placed when GCS object already disappeared.
3. **Enforce a monotonic shared deletion fence** on every writer, including GCS-native delete permissions, rather than trusting a last-moment Firestore read. A generation CAS alone does not serialize a new legal hold; no test callback acts as the production decision.
4. **Test the exact adversarial interleaving**: Firestore authorization complete → hold/revocation requested and committed → GCS physical delete attempt. Require **no unauthorized deletion**, with delayed work, retry/crash, 404/412, different tenants, overlapping legal holds, expired leases and re-created objects.
5. Require one **fully integrated and reviewed final SHA**, passing CI and both emulator and approved Cloud staging negative tests, with AppSec/DPO/Legal/SRE/Release Owner signatures and live Cloud IAM evidence. Only then consider a controlled production GO.
